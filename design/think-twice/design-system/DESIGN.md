## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a calm, deliberate, and emotionally grounding approach to personal wealth and daily consumption. Moving far away from frantic market tickers, neon gamification, and anxiety-inducing red alert banners, the visual language establishes a sanctuary for conscious financial decision-making. 

The aesthetic marries **Refined Contemporary Minimalism** with subtle **Warm Tactility**. It balances the structural rigor and precision required of financial tooling with organic warmth, breathable canvas compositions, and soft material depth. By pairing deep, contemplative slate blues with warm architectural alabasters and selective ochre accents, the interface evokes the feel of a quiet studio workspace, open linen journals, and archival stone.

### Experience Principles
- **Clarity Over Velocity:** Interactions emphasize reflection over instant gratification. Data is parsed and paced gracefully without overwhelming dense clusters.
- **Intentional Restraint:** Color is applied semantically and sparingly; attention is treated as a finite resource.
- **Organic Precision:** Sharp analytical clarity meets soft, rounded corners and tactile paper-like planar layering.

## Colors

The color palette centers on meditative, mineral tones designed to lower visual stress and encourage measured reflection. 

- **Primary (`#334155` - Slate):** Anchors core actions, key summaries, primary buttons, and navigational anchors. Supported by `#1e293b` for high-contrast deep text and `#475569` for secondary structure.
- **Secondary (`#d97706` - Warm Ochre):** Applied selectively as an illuminated spark for goal milestones, budget caution alerts, key highlights, and active micro-indicators. Balanced with deep amber (`#b45309`) for text legs and soft amber wash (`#fef3c7`) for contextual badges.
- **Tertiary (`#475569` - Muted Slate):** Utilized for structural division, iconography, secondary interactive triggers, and metadata.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#faf9f5`, `#f5f3ec`, `#ede9df`):** A warm, parchment-inspired stepped spectrum that serves as the root canvas, card surfaces, and subtle boundary tints, replacing cold clinical grays with living, tactile warmth.

### Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#faf9f5`
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Modules):** `#ffffff`
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Subtle Insets & Wells):** `#f5f3ec`
- **Dividers & Hairlines:** `#ede9df`
- **Primary Text:** `#1e293b`
- **Muted Text:** `#64748b`

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites **Manrope** and **JetBrains Mono** to bridge humanistic clarity with mathematical discipline.

- **Manrope** provides a balanced, open geometric presence that remains warm and legible across scale. Its wide apertures and soft curves complement the mindful aesthetic, ensuring that financial figures, transaction narratives, and guided exercises feel inviting rather than clinical.
- **JetBrains Mono** is introduced selectively for financial ledger lines, categorical tags, timestamps, percentages, and data attribution. The monospaced cadence injects authentic financial intentionality, grounding speculative thoughts with exact numbers.

### Typographic Rules
- Use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across all data displays to maintain horizontal scanability.
- Reserve tight negative letter-spacing strictly for display scales above 20px; small utility labels should be tracked open with moderate letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is grounded in generous breathing room, structured across an 8-point harmonic grid. White space functions as a cognitive decompression buffer between decisions.

### Layout Matrix
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px max container width, 48px canvas outer margin, 24px column gutter. Ensures interfaces never stretch beyond comfortable focal points.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid layout up to 1024px, 32px canvas margin, 20px column gutter.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single stack flow, 20px canvas margin, 16px column gutter. Complex multi-column transaction logs flatten into modular sequential cards.

### Spatial Cadence
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled visual groups (icons alongside values, chip inner buffers).
- Use `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px) for inner card padding and modular field separators.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) to distinguish contextual mental shifts: separating primary balance metrics from daily journaling, or monthly projections from immediate actions.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain visual serenity, elevation is communicated through **Tonal Surface Stacking** and **Ambient Diffuse Whispers**, omitting harsh drop shadows and heavy bordering.

### Material Architecture
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#faf9f5` warm, flat, grounding parchment.
- **Level 1 (Default Card / Container):** `#ffffff` solid, framed by a soft hairline outline (`1px solid #ede9df`). Elevated slightly by an ambient diffuse shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays, Modals, Menus):** Pure white float with a multi-layered warm shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.08), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.02)`.
- **Sunken Wells (Inputs, Inactive buckets):** `#f5f3ec` with zero shadow, bordered by `#ede9df` to convey a carved, recessed surface.

Blur filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with 85% opacity `#faf9f5` are reserved exclusively for sticky top navigation headers, allowing content to dissolve smoothly as the user navigates.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a curated **Rounded (`2`)** standard to communicate approachability, comfort, and considered modernism without sliding into juvenile circular forms.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger targets, badges, and inline data cells.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Standard operational cards, modular spending summaries, and reflection dialogs.
- **Hero Containers (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem / 24px):** Large summary dashboards, seasonal financial review sheets, and onboarding surfaces.
- **Data Pills & Accent Caps (`9999px`):** Status flags, financial tag chips, and micro-metric badges to provide geometric counterpoints against rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep slate blue (`#334155`) background, `#ffffff` text, subtle hover shift to `#1e293b`. `0.5rem` border radius, padding `12px 24px`. Confident, calm, authoritative.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#f5f3ec`, border `1px solid #ede9df`, text `#334155`. Hover shifts to `#ffffff` with a subtle lift.
- **Accent / Intentional Action:** Ochre base (`#d97706`) with pure white text, strictly reserved for decisive positive confirmation (e.g., "Commit to Goal", "Log Conscious Expense").
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, `#475569` text, soft hover tint to `rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.05)`.

### Cards & Modules
Constructed on `#ffffff` with `16px` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii and a clean `1px solid #ede9df` outline. Cards feature generous inner padding (`24px`). When interactive, cards transition border colors smoothly to `#cbd5e1` on hover with a micro-elevation gain.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips:** Light warm sand `#f5f3ec` background with `#475569` text, `rounded-full`, padding `4px 12px`.
- **Mindful Alert Badges:** Ochre highlight (`#fef3c7`) fill with deep amber (`#b45309`) mono-styled label for paused spend or review-needed notifications.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #ede9df`, corner radius `8px`, typography `14px Manrope`.
- Active focus state features an intentional `1.5px` border in `#334155` alongside a diffuse focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.10)`), with no jarring default system blue.
- Labels are rendered above the field in uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`label-sm`) with slate color `#64748b`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
Custom rounded controls (`4px` for checkboxes, circular for radios). Inactive states use a `#ede9df` border and `#ffffff` fill. Selected states fill with `#334155` displaying a crisp white micro-mark.

### Specialized Mindfulness Components
- **The "Pause Threshold" Modal:** A full-focus overlay that blurs the background canvas, presenting a calm space with a warm ochre ambient glow to evaluate unplanned impulse transactions against monthly savings intent.
- **Conscious Log Row:** Transaction list rows featuring two lines: top line displays payee in `Manrope` (`title-md`) alongside mono amount in `#1e293b`; bottom line carries an optional emotional reflection tag (e.g., "Aligned", "Frivolous", "Nourishing") formatted via small muted chips.
